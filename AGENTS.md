# Project Instructions

## Purpose

This website is Felipe Públio's digital business card. It should let visitors
quickly identify Felipe, understand his professional focus, and reach the main
places where they can find or contact him.

It is not intended to be a complete portfolio, résumé, blog, or sales landing
page.

## Product Direction

- Keep the experience simple, technical, direct, and trustworthy.
- Prioritize identity, professional role, external profiles, résumé, and contact.
- Keep copy concise and retain an element only when it has a clear purpose.
- Preserve a personal technical identity without using generic landing-page
  patterns, promotional metrics, card grids, or decorative effects.
- Maintain comfortable navigation and legibility on small and large screens.

## Implementation

- Keep the site lightweight and dependency-free unless a dependency has a clear
  benefit that justifies its cost.
- Use semantic HTML and accessible interaction states.
- Validate responsive behavior after visual changes.
- Do not replace real profile information or links with mock content.
